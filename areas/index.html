What we work on

Industrial rotating machinery, not a single machine type

EchoShift can investigate suitable electric motors, pumps, gearboxes, mixers, agitators, fans, blowers, conveyors, compressors and other rotating equipment where useful vibration measurements can be taken safely.

The diagnostic is scoped around the actual maintenance question, accessible measurement points and operating condition.
