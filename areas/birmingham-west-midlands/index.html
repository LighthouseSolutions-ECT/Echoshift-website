Local vibration analysis for maintenance and engineering teams

When a production machine changes, the useful question is not simply how much it vibrates. EchoShift measures the motor and relevant driven components, records the operating condition and compares vibration across positions so the result can support a maintenance decision.

Typical jobs include one-off motor checks, motor-pump or motor-gearbox investigations, bearing-focused measurements, post-maintenance comparisons and baseline readings on important assets.

Standalone diagnostic service

A diagnostic snapshot stands alone. If the issue is intermittent or the asset is critical enough to justify trend data, continuous condition monitoring can be scoped as a separate next step.

Single-machine diagnostic snapshots start from £125, with the scope and price confirmed before attendance.

Questions

Birmingham vibration diagnostics FAQs

Useful details before arranging a site visit.

Can you check one motor or several machines on the same visit?

Yes. A visit can be scoped around one problem asset or a small group of machines where comparing them under similar operating conditions adds value.

Do you need permanent sensors for a diagnostic snapshot?

No permanent installation is required for a snapshot. Temporary magnetic mounting is practical on compatible rigid surfaces, with other engineered fixing methods available where needed.

Can you compare a machine before and after maintenance?

Yes. Repeat measurements can be useful after bearing, coupling, alignment or other work when the operating condition and measurement positions are kept comparable.
