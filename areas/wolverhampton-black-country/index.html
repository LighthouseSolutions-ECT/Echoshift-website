Practical machine diagnostics for Black Country industry

EchoShift is set up for focused site work on existing plant: identify the machine causing concern, measure the rotating train at useful rigid points, and give maintenance an evidence-led next action.

That can mean separating a motor issue from a gearbox or driven component, checking whether bearing evidence is localised, comparing similar machines, or confirming how vibration changed after maintenance.

The snapshot service works for one-off problem assets as well as multi-machine visits. Fixed monitoring is available when repeated evidence will add practical maintenance value.

Questions

Black Country vibration diagnostics FAQs

Practical answers before booking a machine check.

Can you assess a motor and gearbox together?

Yes. Measuring both sides of the drive train can help separate a motor source from gearbox, coupling or driven-machine vibration.

Do you cover Tipton, Dudley, Wednesbury and West Bromwich?

Yes. EchoShift covers those areas along with Wolverhampton and surrounding Black Country industrial sites, subject to normal site access and job scope.

Can snapshot measurements be taken without drilling?

Usually, yes. A rigid magnetic mount is suitable for many temporary measurements on compatible ferromagnetic surfaces, with existing engineered fixing points used where they provide a better measurement path.
