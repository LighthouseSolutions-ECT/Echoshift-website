Asset-specific vibration analysis

Scope the diagnostic around your machine

Industrial sites contain plenty of rotating equipment that does not fit neatly into a motor, pump, fan or conveyor page. The useful question is whether the machine offers a safe, rigid measurement path and whether vibration can contribute meaningful evidence to the maintenance decision.

Send the machine details, photos or nameplate information and what has changed. EchoShift can confirm the likely measurement points and whether a snapshot, comparison survey or longer monitoring approach makes sense.

Useful for bespoke and less common assets

The scope can be built around bearing-supported shafts, rotary packages, selected spindles, exhausters, separators and other rotating assemblies where vibration is an appropriate diagnostic tool.

What we investigate

Vibration evidence tied to the machine

Frequency content is tied to shaft speed, bearing locations, drive relationships and operating state so the analysis reflects the mechanics of the specific machine.

01

Bearing condition

Accessible bearing housings can be assessed for overall, spectral and higher-frequency vibration change.

02

Rotating-force behaviour

Running-speed and harmonic vibration can help identify imbalance, eccentricity or other periodic forcing mechanisms.

03

Coupling and drive train

Motor, belt, coupling, gearbox or driven-shaft measurements can be compared across a bespoke train.

04

Looseness and impacts

Impulsive or harmonic behaviour can reveal poor mounting, looseness or a compromised load path.

05

Structural response

Frames, housings and supports can amplify vibration and need to be separated from the original forcing source.

06

Operating-state effects

Speed, product, load and duty cycle can be recorded so comparisons remain meaningful.

Measurement strategy

Scope the measurement points before attendance

Machine details, photographs and operating information are used to identify likely rigid points close to bearings, shafts and load paths. This helps define an efficient visit with measurements focused on the maintenance question.

On-site vibration analysis: measurements are taken while the machine is operating in a representative condition where safe access allows, with the operating condition recorded alongside the readings.

Questions

Other rotating machinery FAQs

Practical answers to common questions before an on-site diagnostic.

What information should I send before booking?

A machine description, site location, photographs or nameplate details, operating speed if known, and a short description of what has changed are usually enough to start scoping the visit.

What machines can EchoShift assess?

The method is aimed at industrial rotating machinery with accessible, safe measurement points. Motors, pumps, gearboxes, mixers, fans, conveyors and compressors are common examples, but bespoke rotating equipment can also be scoped.

What if vibration is not the right test?

EchoShift will still use the maintenance question to identify the most useful next step. If another inspection or measurement would add more value, that can be recommended rather than forcing a vibration-only answer.
