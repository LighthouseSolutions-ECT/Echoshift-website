From problem to next step

How EchoShift handles an enquiry

  1. Tell us what changed

    Tell us about the machine, the symptoms and the impact on production. We’ll gather the practical details so you only need to explain the problem once.

  2. EchoShift qualifies the work

    We assess the enquiry and consider EchoShift-direct delivery first. If more information is needed to understand the job, we’ll ask before deciding the next step.

  3. Agree the right next step

    Where appropriate, EchoShift handles the work directly. We confirm the scope, timing and price with you before any work is booked.

If specialist or regional engineering support is appropriate, EchoShift identifies suitable capability. Anyone we introduce must first be vetted and approved for the relevant work, including its requirements and limitations. We agree the commercial route and ask your permission before sharing necessary details with a named specialist. Availability is confirmed for the particular job; an enquiry is not automatically distributed.

A practical conversation

What we’ll ask

A short description is enough to start. Share what you know; EchoShift can follow up on anything that needs clarification.

  • The machinery or asset type and how many assets are involved.
  • The site location or postcode.
  • The symptoms, what changed and any urgency or production impact.
  • Your preferred timing and callback or contact details.
