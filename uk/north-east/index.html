Tyneside engineering and site machinery

For Newcastle, Gateshead and North and South Tyneside, explain whether the equipment is installed at your site or is being worked on for a customer. A workshop observation and an installed-machine investigation may need different information and access arrangements.

If a problem has appeared after installation, identify changes in mounting, connected equipment or operating duty that your team knows about. We can agree the investigation without treating any one change as a confirmed cause.

What to include in the first discussion

  • Machine type, asset count and the site postcode.
  • The operating state in which the symptom occurs.
  • Known speed, load, production-cycle timing and any recent changes.
  • Whether the equipment can be accessed during that state.
  • Induction, permit or specialist requirements, and the person who can clarify them.
  • Your maintenance question, urgency and preferred callback time.

Questions about access and operating state

Should we shut the machine down before an enquiry?

An enquiry does not require you to change the machine's operating state. Explain the current situation and follow your site's operating procedures. We discuss the conditions needed for any proposed work before it is arranged.

Can you work during a planned outage?

Tell us what will be stopped, what can run and what you want to learn. Some questions need operating information, so an outage is not automatically the right window for every diagnostic task.

Does this include specialist process or offshore work?

No such capability is implied. State the requirement and we will establish whether an appropriate route can be offered before confirming any work or specialist involvement.
