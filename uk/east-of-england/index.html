A useful operating record

  • Site postcode, machine type, asset reference and asset count.
  • What you notice and whether it happens during startup, steady running or a change in duty.
  • Known speed, load or process setting at that time.
  • Product, throughput or maintenance changes that coincide with the concern.
  • Previous readings or notes, if available, and the next comparable running window.
  • Access requirements, the maintenance decision and your callback details.

Questions about changing operating conditions

Can seasonal machinery be considered for monitoring?

Yes. Tell us when it operates and what you need to learn. We assess whether an ongoing managed arrangement is appropriate or whether a defined check during a useful run better matches the requirement.

Are two vibration readings always directly comparable?

No. The machine's duty and the way measurements were taken matter. Supply the context you have so the basis of any comparison can be agreed.

What if we have no earlier readings?

You can still describe the concern. We can discuss an initial investigation and what information would make later comparisons useful, without claiming an existing baseline that has never been measured.
