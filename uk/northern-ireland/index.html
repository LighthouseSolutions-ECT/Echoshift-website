Vibration analysis for installed and workshop machinery

Northern Ireland's engineering and materials-handling businesses may need help with their own plant or with equipment they make or maintain for a customer. Identify the affected motor and driven assembly, then say where the symptom occurs and under what load.

A snapshot provides focused measurements, interpretation and a findings summary for the agreed equipment. An unloaded workshop run may not reproduce vibration seen in an installed conveyor or drive. We explain what the proposed measurement can address and what would need to be checked at the operating site.

See vibration diagnostics, gearbox diagnostics and conveyor drive diagnostics. Give the actual installed location when asking for a quotation so we can scope the assessment accurately.

Belfast Antrim and the eastern counties

For Belfast, Newtownabbey, Carrickfergus, Lisburn and Antrim, identify the equipment's owner and the contact able to authorise access. Include Down, Ards and North Down enquiries through this route.

For Ballymena, Larne and the Causeway coast, state whether the concern is at a production site, workshop or another installed location. If the machine belongs to a customer, clarify where the work would take place before requesting a quotation.

Mid Ulster and the southern manufacturing corridor

For Dungannon, Cookstown, Magherafelt, Armagh, Craigavon, Portadown, Banbridge and Newry, describe the drive and the task it performs. If a machine handles material, include the operating load and when the symptom is noticed.

The useful question is not simply whether a drive can run, but whether the proposed operating arrangement represents the concern. Send any specifications or earlier observations already available. Motor vibration diagnostics and pump diagnostics explain those asset pathways.

North west and western sites

For Derry/Londonderry, Strabane, Limavady, Fermanagh and Omagh, provide the full installed-site postcode and a contact familiar with the machinery. Distinguish the collection or workshop address from the place where the symptom occurs.

If timing is important, give the date of the maintenance decision and the next useful running window. We agree the diagnostic scope and attendance timing with you before booking.

Prepare the brief before travel is quoted

  • Asset type, reference and the number of machines involved.
  • Installed location and postcode, plus any separate workshop address.
  • Whether you operate, manufacture or maintain the equipment for someone else.
  • Symptom, duty, known speed or load and recent changes.
  • Available run window, access requirements and authorising contact.
  • Urgency, the decision required and your preferred callback time.

The first call can establish missing information. It is a qualification conversation, not a substitute for measurements needed to diagnose a machine.

Questions about location and delivery

Can we enquire before we know whether travel is worthwhile?

Yes. Give us the machinery brief and the decision you need to make. We can establish what information and practical arrangements would be needed before a visit or another suitable route is proposed.

Does a manufacturing enquiry include testing a product for certification?

No certification or product-approval service is implied. Explain the particular machinery concern and any formal requirement so suitability can be checked before work is accepted.

Does this page also promise service in the Republic of Ireland?

No. This page is for Northern Ireland enquiries. If the equipment is elsewhere, state its actual country and location so we can respond accurately about what, if anything, can be offered.
