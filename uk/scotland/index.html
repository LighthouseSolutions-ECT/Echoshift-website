Questions about records and repeat work

Do we need a complete asset register before calling?

No. Start with the affected machine and the information you have. We can identify what else is needed to define that enquiry without making a full-site register a condition of the first conversation.

Can readings from before a component replacement be reused?

Keep them as history and explain what changed. Whether they provide a useful comparison depends on the assembly, operating duty and measurement context. We will not treat them as an unchanged baseline automatically.

Can the same arrangement cover several Scottish sites?

List the sites and the purpose at each. We agree access, suitable assets and the scope of ongoing work for each location.
