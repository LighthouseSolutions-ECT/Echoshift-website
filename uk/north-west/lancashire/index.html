Build monitoring around asset priority

For an engineering business with several machines, a useful condition-monitoring discussion starts with criticality, duty and access. It does not require every asset to be treated alike. EchoShift can qualify a focused initial investigation and discuss a wider programme once the priorities are clear.

The authoritative service detail is on vibration diagnostics. Application pages cover electric motors, pumps, fans and blowers, geared drives and conveyors. Use the relevant page to describe the assembly without needing to identify the fault yourself.

A useful result from a single-machine investigation

A snapshot stands on its own as an engineering diagnostic service: focused vibration measurements, interpretation and a clear findings summary with a recommended next step. You can use that evidence for the immediate maintenance discussion; ongoing monitoring is a separate option when a trend would add value.

What to gather before the conversation

  • The site postcode, machine name and the job or process it supports.
  • Approximate running pattern, normal duty and whether the symptom is recurring or new.
  • Recent repairs, lubrication or component changes, plus any previous readings.
  • The person who knows the machine, a callback number and available maintenance windows.

EchoShift supports Lancashire sites including Preston, Blackburn, Burnley, Accrington, Lancaster and the surrounding industrial areas. If your business operates from several workshops or factories, send the addresses separately and identify which one needs attention first. Agreeing asset access and a site contact in advance helps make a planned investigation useful.
