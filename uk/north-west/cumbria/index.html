Prioritise what needs evidence before the next maintenance window

Process-support pumps and fans

Identify which rotating assets are essential to the operating process and whether there is usable standby capacity. Include duty, speed where known and the change that raised concern. Scope can then focus on the assets that matter most to the maintenance plan.

Gearboxes and handling machinery

A defined snapshot diagnostic may suit a single fault investigation or post-maintenance question. Use the findings to plan what follows

The diagnostic findings summary explains what the measurements support and the next step recommended by the evidence. If further measurements would materially improve the decision, that is identified. For a planned site visit, this helps the maintenance team distinguish the question answered from any follow-up work still needed.

Prepare for a productive site visit

  • Site postcode, entrance or gate instructions and the person authorising access.
  • Priority asset list, symptoms and the consequence of each asset being unavailable.
  • Operating or shutdown windows, induction time, permits and escort requirements.
  • Existing readings and repair history, plus the contact who will discuss the findings and next steps.

Coverage includes Carlisle, Workington, Whitehaven, Barrow-in-Furness, Kendal, Penrith and other Cumbrian industrial locations. For multiple or separated sites, give each location its own access details and priority list. Travel and scheduling are planned with the work, rather than assuming that one arrival time or induction covers every site.
