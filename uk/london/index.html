Questions about shared sites and machinery enquiries

Can a facilities manager make the initial enquiry?

Yes. Tell us which machine is involved, who controls it and what information is available. We can clarify the scope and the person needed to authorise access before arranging work.

Can you investigate a neighbour's noise complaint?

This page offers industrial machinery diagnostics, not a general noise or building-vibration survey. If a specific machine under your control is suspected, describe it so we can establish whether a machinery investigation is appropriate.

Does the enquiry line mean an engineer can attend tonight?

No. You can leave the machinery details through the 24-hour enquiry line. We agree the response, scope and any attendance separately after qualification.
