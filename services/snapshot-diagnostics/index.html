When to use it

Useful when something has changed

Use a snapshot to investigate a change, assess a suspected component or compare machine condition before and after maintenance.

01

New or increasing vibration

A machine feels rougher than normal, operators have noticed a change, or an existing vibration reading has risen.

02

Noise or mechanical concern

A motor, bearing housing, gearbox or driven component sounds different and you want evidence before intervention.

03

Bearing concern

There is reason to suspect rolling-element bearing deterioration and the machine offers a usable measurement path.

04

Post-maintenance check

You want to compare the machine after bearing, coupling, alignment or other maintenance work.

05

Comparison between machines

Two nominally similar assets behave differently and you want a structured vibration comparison.

06

Before continuous monitoring

You want to establish whether the asset justifies a longer monitored deployment.

Rotating machinery

Diagnostics across the rotating machine

For snapshot work, a rigid magnetic sensor mount can provide a fast, repeatable no-drill measurement on suitable ferromagnetic surfaces. Existing threaded points or stud mounting may be used where appropriate. Mounting quality, surface condition and position are treated as part of the measurement — especially for higher-frequency bearing evidence.

On-site process

From maintenance question to usable evidence

The visit stays focused on the decision you need to make.

01

Define the question

Confirm the machine, symptom, operating condition, access and what decision the measurement needs to support.

02

Capture vibration

Take measurements at relevant rigid locations, record orientation and operating context, and use comparison points where useful.

03

Analyse the evidence

Review overall vibration, spectral and harmonic behaviour, bearing-focused evidence where appropriate, and agreement between measurements.

04

Report the next action

Summarise the main finding, the confidence behind it and the most useful next maintenance or measurement step.

Questions

Snapshot diagnostic FAQs

Practical answers before you book.

How much does a diagnostic snapshot cost?

A single-machine diagnostic snapshot starts from £125. Multi-machine visits, extended travel or more involved diagnostic work are quoted before attendance so you know the scope and price in advance.

Do you need to drill the machine?

Usually not for a snapshot. A suitable rigid magnetic mount is the preferred fast option on compatible surfaces. Other temporary or existing fixing points can be used where they give a better measurement path.

What can one diagnostic visit tell us?

A single visit can often identify strong vibration patterns, localise the area of concern and direct the next maintenance step. Some problems benefit from speed, load, geometry, repeat measurements or inspection before the final cause is confirmed.

What machinery can you assess?

EchoShift can assess electric motors, pumps, gearboxes, mixers, fans, blowers, conveyors, compressors and other rotating machinery where safe, useful vibration measurements can be taken.
